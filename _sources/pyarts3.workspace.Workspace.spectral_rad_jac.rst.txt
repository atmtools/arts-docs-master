spectral_rad_jac
================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.spectral_rad_jac
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.StokvecMatrix

   Jacobian of :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad` with respect to :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.jac_targets`.

   The size of this variable should be the local :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.jac_targets` as rows times the
   size of the local :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad` as columns.
