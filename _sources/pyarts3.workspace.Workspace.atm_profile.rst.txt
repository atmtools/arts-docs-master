atm_profile
===========

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.atm_profile
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.ArrayOfAtmPoint

   An atmospheric profile in ARTS.

   This exists to interface between the fully 3D atmospheric field native to ARTS
   and various 1D and 2D solvers that make use of profiles for fixed geometries.

   The atmospheric profile consists of all the relevant atmospheric field data
   at a discrete profile in the atmosphere.  It is often extracted from an :class:`~pyarts3.arts.AtmField`
   at a single latitude-longitude coordinate but may of course be generated manually.

   See :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.atm_field` for the data that may be available in the atmospheric point.

   The size of the profile is the same as :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.alt_grid`.

   For more information, see :doc:`user.atm_field`.
