spectral_nlte_srcvec
====================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.spectral_nlte_srcvec
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.StokvecVector

   The part of the source vector that is due to non-LTE.

   This is closely related to :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_propmat`.

   Gven the level source term:

   .. math:: \vec{J} = \mathbf{K}^{-1} \left(\vec{\alpha}B + \vec{J}_n + \cdots\right),

   this variable holds :math:`\vec{J}_n`.  Here, :math:`\vec{\alpha}` is the first
   column of :math:`\mathbf{K}`, which is from the :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_propmat` variable.
   :math:`B` is the Planck function.  The ellipsis denotes other terms that can
   come from more sources, such as scattering and/or transmitting equipment.

   The unit is in :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad` per meter.
