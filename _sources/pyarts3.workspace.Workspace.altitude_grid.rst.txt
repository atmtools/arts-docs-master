altitude_grid
=============

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.altitude_grid
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.AscendingGrid
   
   An ascending list of :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.altitude`.  Often related to a field or a profile.
   
   Unit: m
   
   .. note::
       There is no global grid system in ARTS, so beware of the local
       nature of all grids.
