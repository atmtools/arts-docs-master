spectral_rad_closed_surface_agenda
==================================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.spectral_rad_closed_surface_agenda
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.Agenda

   A closed surface agenda.

   It behave exactly like :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda`.  It exists
   to allow chaining surface agendas.  The idea is that the main
   :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda` variable is the first interface
   and can chain into another surface agenda - this one.

   Thus this agenda must be "closed".  It cannot call another :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda`,
   whereas :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda` can call this agenda.  Imagine a chain where
   the :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda` gets the reflectance from a land surface model
   and calls the :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_observer_agenda` to compute the downwelling radiation at the surface.
   It can in turn call :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.spectral_rad_closed_surface_agenda` to get the upwelling radiation from the surface
   that is being emitted.  That's the type of use case this agenda is made for and why it exists!
