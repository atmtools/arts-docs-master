atm_disort_settings_agenda
==========================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.atm_disort_settings_agenda
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.Agenda

   A specialization of :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.disort_settings_agenda` for atmospheric calculations.

   .. seealso::
       :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.subsurf_disort_settings_agenda` for a similar agenda for subsurface calculations..

   .. rubric:: Execution and customization


   You can execute :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.atm_disort_settings_agenda` directly from the workspace by calling :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.atm_disort_settings_agendaExecute`.

   As all agendas in ARTS, it is also customizable via its operator helper class: :class:`~pyarts3.arts.DisortSettingsAgendaOperator`.
   See it, :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.atm_disort_settings_agendaSetOperator`, and :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.atm_disort_settings_agendaExecuteOperator` for more details.

   Also see the :class:`~pyarts3.arts_agenda` property for how to fully define an agenda in python.
