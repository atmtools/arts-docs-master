lon_grid
========

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.lon_grid
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.LonGrid

   An ascending list of :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.lon`.  Often related to a field or a profile.

   Units: degrees

   .. note::
       There is no global grid system in ARTS, so beware of the local
       nature of all grids.
