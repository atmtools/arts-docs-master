latitude_grid
=============

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.latitude_grid
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.LatGrid
   
   An ascending list of :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.latitude`.  Often related to a field or a profile.
   
   Units: degrees
   
   .. note::
       There is no global grid system in ARTS, so beware of the local
       nature of all grids.
