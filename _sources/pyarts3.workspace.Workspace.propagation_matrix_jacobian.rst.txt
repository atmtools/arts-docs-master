propagation_matrix_jacobian
===========================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.propagation_matrix_jacobian
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.PropmatMatrix
   
   Partial derivative of the :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.propagation_matrix` with regards to :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.jacobian_targets`.
   
   The units depend on what is set in :attr:`~pyarts3.workspace.Workspace.jacobian_targets` [1 / m / jacobian target's unit].
