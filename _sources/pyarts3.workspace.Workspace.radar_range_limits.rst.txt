radar_range_limits
==================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.radar_range_limits
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.Matrix

   Range-gate limits for active-radar measurements.

   The matrix has one row per element of :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.measurement_sensor` and two columns
   holding the lower and upper edge.  The coordinate represented by the limits is
   selected by the radar forward method (altitude, one-way distance, or
   round-trip time).
