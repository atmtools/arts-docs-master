absorption_lookup_table
=======================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.absorption_lookup_table
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.AbsorptionLookupTables

   Absorption lookup table for scalar gas absorption coefficients.

   Precomputing this table replaces the need for the calculation of scalar gas line-by-line
   absorption.
