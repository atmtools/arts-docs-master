spectral_rad_surface_agenda
===========================

.. currentmodule:: pyarts3.workspace

.. attribute:: Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda
   :type: ~pyarts3.arts.Agenda

   Computes spectral radiance as seen of the surface.

   This agenda calculates the spectral radiance as seen of the surface.
   One common use-case us to provide a background spectral radiance.

   The input path point should be as if it is looking at the surface.

   Subsurface calculations are also supported through this agenda,
   but might require setting :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_closed_surface_agenda`
   as well.

   .. rubric:: Execution and customization

   See :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agendaSet` for builtin options that selects execution options.

   You can execute :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agenda` directly from the workspace by calling :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agendaExecute`.

   As all agendas in ARTS, it is also customizable via its operator helper class: :class:`~pyarts3.arts.SpectralRadianceSurfaceAgendaOperator`.
   See it, :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agendaSetOperator`, and :func:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_surface_agendaExecuteOperator` for more details.

   Also see the :class:`~pyarts3.arts_agenda` property for how to fully define an agenda in python.

   .. rubric:: Constraints

   #. On output, :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad` has the size of :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.freq_grid`.
   #. On output, :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.spectral_rad_jac` has the shape of the expected :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.model_state_vec` (i.e., the x-size of :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.jac_targets`) times the size of :attr:`~pyarts3.Workspace.freq_grid`.
