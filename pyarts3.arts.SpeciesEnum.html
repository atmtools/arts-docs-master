SpeciesEnum

class pyarts3.arts.SpeciesEnum(*args, **kwargs)

The valid species for the ARTS system.

Valid options:

  • "Bath" or "AIR": Any non-descript species

  • "Water" or "H2O": Water molecule

  • "CarbonDioxide" or "CO2": Carbon Dioxide molecule
